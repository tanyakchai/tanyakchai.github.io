How I work

A few things I hold myself to

Clarity before building

I start by understanding the problem, the people affected, and what a successful outcome needs to look like. Requirements may evolve, but the work should always have a clear purpose.

Evidence over assumption

I validate requirements, test solutions, and distinguish confirmed experience from inference. If something does not work as intended, I want to find that out before the user does.

Design for the people using it

A technically correct system can still fail if it adds friction. I pay attention to how people actually work and build processes that are practical, understandable, and maintainable.

Own the outcome

I am naturally curious and resourceful. When I encounter an unfamiliar tool or an inefficient process, I learn what I need, ask questions, and keep working until I can move the problem forward.

Keep learning

Technology keeps moving, and I make a point of moving with it. I continuously build my skills, explore new tools, and stay current on emerging capabilities so I can evaluate what is useful and apply it thoughtfully—not simply chase what is new.
