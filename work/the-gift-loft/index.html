The Gift Loft

A curated gift-box storefront — brand identity, Shopify build, product catalog, and social marketing — designed and run solo from the ground up.

Role

Founder, designer, and operator — brand, storefront, and marketing

Tools

Shopify, Canva, Meta Business Suite, Facebook & Instagram Ads

The business

Curated gifting, built as a real storefront — not a side project.

The Gift Loft sells curated gift boxes and individual gifts — ready-made sets, build-your-own boxes, and single items — organized by occasion, recipient, category, and price. The catalog runs across more than a dozen active collections, from teacher-appreciation gifts to game-day gear, each one photographed, merchandised, and kept current solo.

Every part of it, end to end

Not a template with products dropped in — the brand, the storefront, the catalog, and the marketing behind it were all built and are still run by one person.

Brand & visual identity

A flexible identity built for digital and physical use

I developed The Gift Loft's visual identity from the ground up, including the primary wordmark, supporting monogram, neutral color system, packaging pieces, storefront graphics, and campaign creative. The system was designed to feel elevated and recognizable while remaining flexible enough for product launches, seasonal promotions, social content, and the unboxing experience.

Visual palette

Logo variations designed for different placements

I created a flexible logo suite rather than relying on a single lockup. The circular versions work well for profile images, stickers, packaging seals, and other compact spaces, while the monogram provides a simplified mark for small-format applications.

A recognizable motif system

The four-point sparkle was pulled from the letterforms and developed into a supporting motif. It can stand alone as a small brand cue or repeat as a pattern across packaging, inserts, and digital backgrounds.

The brand carried through the customer experience

The identity extends beyond the screen. I created packaging inserts that reinforce the brand, encourage customers to share their purchases, and connect the unboxing experience back to the website and social channels.

Website & storefront

Built and maintained entirely in Shopify

Theme customization, a full collection structure organized by occasion, recipient, category, and price, product setup and photography across the catalog, and an integrated checkout supporting Shop Pay, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
